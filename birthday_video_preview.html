FROM MEG HRAJ. TO DIANA.

A Special Letter ♥

To My Lilliput, Daino & My Favourite Capsicum ♥

My Dear Diana,

Happy Birthday, my Lilliput. ♥

Sometimes the most beautiful people enter our lives quietly. They simply become part of our days, our thoughts, and eventually, our hearts.

We have shared countless calls, laughter, dreams, worries, photographs and silly arguments. Yet the most beautiful part of our story is that we have never even met one-on-one.

We haven't sat together, walked together, or looked into each other's eyes while having these conversations. And still, somehow, you never felt far away.

Maybe distance is measured in kilometres, but closeness is measured in moments.

My Lilliput—you may have a tiny name, but somehow you found a very big place in my heart. My Daino, you became familiar in a way I never expected.

And then there is my favourite Capsicum. 😂♥ Sweet, spicy, slightly dramatic, and somehow impossible not to love.

I wish I could be there today—to hand you this gift, see your expression, and simply say “Happy Birthday” while standing in front of you. For now, this letter has to cross the distance for me. Let it be a small hug from far away.

Someday, when we finally meet, I hope we look back at these days and smile—because before we ever stood beside each other, we somehow became important to each other from a distance.

Happy Birthday, my sweet Diana. ♥

With all my love,
Meghraj ♥

P.S. — One day, I'll give you that birthday hug in person. Until then, keep this letter safe. ♥
